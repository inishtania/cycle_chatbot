# 🩷 CycleSync — Your AI-Powered Menstrual & Wellness Assistant  

**CycleSync** is a simple Streamlit app that helps women understand their menstrual cycle, moods, and energy better.  
It gives personalized daily insights — from nutrition tips to emotional balance — based on your current cycle phase.  

No complicated tracking or medical jargon, just an intuitive chat interface that feels like talking to a personal assistant who *gets* your hormones.  

---

## ✨ Features  

- 🩸 **Cycle Awareness** — Understand which phase (Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulation, or Luteal) you’re in  
- 💬 **AI Chat Assistant** — Ask questions about your symptoms, energy, or mood  
- 🍎 **Personalized Nutrition & Lifestyle Tips** — Get food and habit recommendations that fit your phase  
- 🌼 **Simple User Input** — Just enter your period start date, cycle length, and today’s date  
- 📊 **Interactive Interface** — Clean sidebar and chat layout powered by Streamlit  

---

## 🧠 How It Works  

1. You input basic information about your cycle (start date & length).  
2. The app calculates your current phase.  
3. You can chat with the AI assistant for daily guidance — whether it’s cravings, mood swings, or energy dips.  
4. The AI gives suggestions based on hormonal patterns in that specific phase.  

---

## 🛠️ Built With  

- **Python** 🐍  
- **Streamlit** — for the web app interface  
- **LangChain + OpenAI API** — for AI reasoning and natural conversation  
- **Datetime & Simple Logic** — to calculate the menstrual phase
